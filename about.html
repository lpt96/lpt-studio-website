About

I'm Liam — I design and build software, on my own, for people who need it done well.

LPT Studio is just me: one developer who enjoys building useful, polished digital products, and who'd rather work directly with you than manage a project through layers of account handlers.

I work directly with clients

No handoffs, no account managers — every conversation about your project is with the person actually building it.

Idea through to finished product

I take projects from a first rough conversation all the way through design, build and launch — one person, one thread of responsibility.

Design matters as much as function

A tool that works but looks and feels clumsy still costs you trust. I care about both sides of that equation.

Technology, made accessible

You don't need to know what an API is for me to build you one. I explain things in plain terms and make decisions with you, not around you.

Comfortable working with non-technical teams

Plenty of my clients have never briefed a developer before. That's a normal starting point, not a barrier.

A bit more context

Why "without the agency price tag" actually matters

Agencies carry overhead — account managers, sales teams, office space — that gets built into every quote. Working with one developer directly means that overhead simply isn't there, so more of what you pay goes into the actual project.

Want to talk through an idea?

No pressure, no sales pitch — just a straightforward conversation about what you need.
