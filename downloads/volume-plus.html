Volume+

A volume slider for every app on your Mac. Turn a noisy app down without touching your music, send each app to its own speakers, and finally get working volume keys on monitor speakers.

Download for Mac How to install
Version 2.07.2 MBmacOS 14.4 or laterApple silicon & Intel
Features

The volume controls macOS should have had.

Per-app volume

Every app playing sound gets its own slider and mute — with up to 200% boost for quiet videos and podcasts.

Per-app output

Send Spotify to your speakers and a call to your AirPods at the same time. Each app can have its own output device.

Sound controls

Bass, treble and voice boost for each app. Prefer just volume? Hide them with one switch.

Quieter during calls

Optional: music and videos drop automatically while someone's talking in Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, Discord or Slack.

Presets

Save sets of levels like “Work” or “Gaming” and switch between them in one click — or with a shortcut.

Keyboard shortcuts

Turn the app you're using up, down or mute it from anywhere. Every shortcut can be changed.

Volume for monitor speakers

Speakers in monitors and TVs over HDMI or DisplayPort get a working volume control — and your volume keys work with them.

Pin your favourites

Pinned apps stay at the top, and wait in a Pinned drawer when they're not playing — ready to open in one click.

Menu bar or window

Run it as a window or tucked into the menu bar, with Liquid Glass on macOS Tahoe. Scroll over the icon to change volume.

Up and running in a minute.

Download

Download the .dmg and open it from your Downloads folder.

03

Allow two permissions

Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

macOS calls Volume+'s main permission “System Audio Recording”, which sounds alarming. Here's what it actually means.

Nothing is recorded

Each app's audio is adjusted live and played straight back out. It's never saved, stored or kept.

Your microphone is never used

Volume+ only works with sound your apps are already playing. It never asks for or touches the microphone.

No data collected

No accounts, analytics or tracking. The only thing Volume+ ever connects to is the update check on this website.

The recording dot is normal

macOS shows a recording indicator in the menu bar while Volume+ is adjusting app volumes. That's expected for this permission.

Questions

Good to know.

Why isn't it on the App Store?

Changing other apps' volume needs system-level audio access that the Mac App Store doesn't permit. Volume+ is still signed with my Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple — Apple's own security check for apps distributed outside the App Store.

Is it really free?

Yes — no trial, no ads, no in-app purchases. If it's useful to you, you can buy me a coffee on Ko-fi.

Why does it say to set my speakers to 100%?

Volume+ adjusts volume in software. Setting your speakers or monitor to full gives it the whole range to work with, then you control the level from Volume+.

How do I get updates?

Volume+ 2.0 and later check for updates automatically and offer to install them — no need to come back here. You can also choose “Check for Updates…” from Settings. If you're on 1.0, download 2.0 from this page once.

Can I control each browser tab separately?

Not yet — browsers play every tab's sound through one shared process, so Volume+ sees the browser as a single app.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Volume+ from its menu, then drag it from Applications to the Bin. If you turned on Launch at Login, turn it off in Volume+'s settings first.
