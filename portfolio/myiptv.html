MyIPTV — your own IPTV playlists, properly designed.

An IPTV player for Apple TV, iPhone and iPad. Bring your own playlist; MyIPTV gives it a fast, tidy interface and keeps your setup in sync through iCloud.

The idea My role Design & build Technology Outcome

The problem / idea

Most IPTV players feel like afterthoughts, especially on the TV. MyIPTV starts from the living-room screen and works outwards to the phone and tablet.

My role

Solo design and development, from concept through to App Store release.

Design & development work

What went into it:

  • A tvOS-first interface designed for the remote
  • VLC-based playback for wide format support, including MKV
  • iCloud sync between devices
  • Bring-your-own playlist — no content is provided or hosted
  • Shared codebase across Apple TV, iPhone and iPad

Technology

SwiftUItvOSVLCKitCloudKit

Outcome & current status

The Apple TV version has been approved by App Review and the iPhone and iPad versions are in review. Release is coming soon.
