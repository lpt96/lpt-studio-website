Flexle — a daily word puzzle built from scratch, with a growing social layer.

A native iOS word game with its own word-validation engine, a hint economy, and a full friends-and-groups social system — all backed by a lightweight, self-managed backend.

The problem / idea

Word games are one of the most crowded categories on the App Store, but most daily-word titles stop at the puzzle itself. The idea behind Flexle was to build a genuinely well-made core game, then extend it into something people play with friends rather than alone — without turning it into a bloated, ad-heavy experience.

My role

Solo design and development, end to end — game design, UI, the Swift codebase, backend setup, and ongoing App Store maintenance and updates.

Design & development work

The core game runs on a bundled 60,000+ word list, so validation works instantly offline with no API dependency. On top of that:

  • A hint system with tracking that carries through into shareable result images
  • Drag-and-drop letter entry with a dedicated "fill known" assist
  • A full backend migration to add accounts, friends, groups and league tables
  • Email verification and transactional email for account features
  • Ad integration that respects the player — no tracking, environment-aware display

Outcome & current status

Flexle is live on the App Store and under active development, with the most recent milestone being a complete friends-and-social feature set — player profiles, friend requests, groups, league tables and verified accounts.
