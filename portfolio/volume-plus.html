Volume+ — real per-app volume control for macOS.

A menu bar utility that gives every app its own volume slider — including a 200% boost — built directly on Apple's low-level Core Audio Process Tap API.

Role

Design & development

Platform

macOS 14.4+

Stack

Swift, AppKit, Core Audio

Status
Live · direct download
The idea My role Design & build Technology Outcome

The problem / idea

macOS only offers one system-wide volume control, so there's no easy way to turn down one noisy app while keeping music or a call at full volume. Volume+ fixes that with real per-app control, not a workaround.

My role

Solo design and development, including researching and implementing Apple's newer Core Audio Process Tap API from the ground up.

Design & development work

Built as a plain AppKit app rather than a SwiftUI lifecycle app, for full control over a lightweight menu bar presence. Features include:

  • Per-app sliders with up to 200% volume boost
  • A live equalizer visualization
  • Software master volume for devices with fixed hardware output
  • Keyboard volume key interception and Bluetooth device auto-switching
  • Launch-at-login and app icon theming

Technology

SwiftAppKitCore Audio Process TapStoreKit 2

Outcome & current status

Notarized and distributed as a Developer ID-signed direct download — the Process Tap API isn't compatible with Mac App Store sandboxing, so this ships outside the App Store by design.
