Credits — a personal, beautifully-made film and TV journal.

A journal for everything you've watched — with season-by-season TV tracking, a home screen widget, and Siri shortcuts, wrapped in a custom "Glassy Gold" theme.

The idea My role Design & build Technology Outcome

The problem / idea

Most "watch tracking" apps feel like spreadsheets with icons. Credits started from a different question: what would a film and TV journal feel like if it were designed with the same care as the films it's cataloguing?

My role

Solo design and development — visual design, data model, and the full SwiftUI build.

Design & development work

Recent work spans the visual system and several platform integrations:

  • A custom "Glassy Gold" theme applied consistently across every screen
  • A hybrid timeline view — a horizontal map strip paired with a vertical card list
  • A home screen widget sharing data via an App Group snapshot
  • Siri integration through App Intents
  • Season-by-season TV tracking and franchise/collection browsing

Technology

SwiftUISwiftDataCloudKitWidgetKitApp Intents

Outcome & current status

Still in active development. A "Director's Cut" premium tier is designed and feature-flagged, pending licensing arrangements — a deliberate decision to hold back monetisation until the core experience has real usage behind it.
